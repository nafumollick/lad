05 / FAQ

Answers to what clients usually ask before ordering.

Still unsure about something? Reach out below and I'll answer directly.

What file format will I actually receive?

Every list is delivered as a CSV file and/or a shared Google Sheet — whichever is easier for you to plug into your CRM or outreach tool. Column headers are clearly labeled so the file is usable the moment you open it.

How are the business emails verified?

Each email address is checked for valid formatting, working domain/MX records, and deliverability before it goes into your list — so you're not stuck guessing which contacts are real.

What happens if some contacts bounce or turn out inaccurate?

If any verified email bounces or a contact's details turn out to be wrong within 7 days of delivery, I'll research and send you a free replacement — no extra charge.

Can I customize which fields are included?

Yes. The listed packages include First Name, Last Name, Job Title, Verified Business Email, LinkedIn URL, and Business Name & Location by default, but fields like phone number, company size, or industry can be added — just mention it when you reach out.

How and when do I pay?

We'll agree on scope and price over email first. Payment is due before I start research on paid packages; the free 10-lead sample requires no payment at all.

Is my project information kept confidential?

Yes — your target market, criteria, and any data you share stay confidential and are used only to complete your project. I'm happy to sign an NDA if your company requires one.

What if I need more than 1,000 contacts?

Larger volumes, recurring monthly lists, or multi-market projects are handled through the Monthly Pipeline or Full Project options above — just reach out with your target numbers and I'll send a custom quote.

How fast is delivery, really?

Delivery windows scale with list size: 1 day for Basic (100 leads), 2 days for Standard (250 leads), and 3 days for Premium (500 leads). Larger or more complex projects get a specific timeline when we scope the work together.
