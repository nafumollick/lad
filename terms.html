Terms of service

Last updated: September 2026

These are the general terms for engaging Nafeu Mollick for freelance design work through this site. The specific scope, timeline, and price for any project are set out in a separate written proposal, which takes precedence over this page where the two differ.

Proposals & scope

Work begins once a proposal is agreed in writing. Scope changes after that point are handled as a separate change request, not folded silently into the original price.

Revisions

Each proposal states how many revision rounds are included. Additional rounds can be added by agreement.

Payment

Payment terms (deposit, milestones, or full payment) are set per proposal. Files are delivered on receipt of final payment unless otherwise agreed.

Ownership

Once a project is paid in full, you own the final deliverables. Any third-party assets (stock fonts, licensed images) remain subject to their own licenses.

Cancellation

Either party can end a project in progress; work completed to that point is billed at the agreed rate.

This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice, and isn't a substitute for a signed proposal or contract for a given project.
